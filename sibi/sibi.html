How to use

1. Press the "START" button.
2. If there is a use camer permission pop up, press "Allow".
3. Show a sign to the camera (must be Sistem Isyarat Bahasa Indonesia)
4. The letter will automatically be translated!

This website must use your webcam.

- Use Space or Backspace buttons (or your keyboard) to edit text.
- You can disable the auto confirm by clicking its button if you don't like it.
- To translate manually, press the "Confirm" button (or enter on your keyboard).
- To change translation mode, simply click the options above the tutorial button.

Note:
J uses the letter I, tilted 135 degrees to the right (facing bottom-left/the battery icon on your taskbar)
For Z, point your finger upwards and tilt 45 degrees to the left.
